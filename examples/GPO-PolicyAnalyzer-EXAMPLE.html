This compares configured values across GPOs (like Microsoft Policy Analyzer) — it is not effective/resultant policy. OU scope, block inheritance, link-enforcement interplay, security filtering and WMI filters are not evaluated, so a flagged conflict may not actually collide on a given object. Open a setting and use Link precedence & where applied to judge real overlap.
